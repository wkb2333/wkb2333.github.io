伦敦国王学院硕士课题（2026.04 -- 2026.08）。把长程机器人行为看成潜空间技能序列：RQ-VAE 码本、causal Transformer 高层规划、conditional diffusion 底层动作。厨房仿真、9 自由度机械臂上，长程完成率高于传统规划器及部分 VLA baseline。

King's College London master's project (Apr. 2026 -- Aug. 2026). Long-horizon robot behavior as sequential latent-skill modeling. On a kitchen simulator with a 9-DoF arm, long-horizon success rates exceeded classical planners and selected VLA baselines.
