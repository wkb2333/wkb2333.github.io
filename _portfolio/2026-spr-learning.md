King's College London master's project (Apr. 2026 -- Aug. 2026).

Long-horizon robot behavior is treated as sequential latent-skill modeling: an RQ-VAE codebook, a causal Transformer for high-level plans, and conditional diffusion for low-level actions. On a kitchen simulator with a 9-DoF arm, long-horizon success rates exceeded classical planners and selected VLA baselines.
