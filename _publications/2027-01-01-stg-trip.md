Addresses sparse target-city trajectories, non-transferable geographic coordinates, and spatiotemporal trip constraints, while hometown check-ins are relatively abundant.

**Method.** Distance-preserving spatiotemporal embeddings and a gating network inject distance similarity; hometown preferences are transferred via user embeddings.

**Result.** Improves F1 by about 18% over SOTA models on average across datasets and baselines.

Only student author; led the method, experimental design, evaluation, and writing.
