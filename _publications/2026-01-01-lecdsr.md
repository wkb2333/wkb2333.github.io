Undergraduate thesis. Addresses sparsity, cold start, and missing item semantics in cross-domain sequential recommendation.

**Method.** Use LLM priors to generate cross-domain user profiles and item semantic embeddings, then align ID and semantic spaces with Fourier fusion and contrastive learning.

**Result.** Improves MRR, NDCG, and HR by 20% on average over SOTA models across multiple datasets.

Third author and the only student author; led the method, experiments, and first draft.
