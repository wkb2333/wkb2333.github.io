Short English notes will live here: paper summaries, course write-ups, and experiment logs.

Chinese notes are under **中文 → 博客**. Each post sets `lang: en` or `lang: zh` in the front matter so it only appears in that language’s blog list.

To add a post, create `_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.
